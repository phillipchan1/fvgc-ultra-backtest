# Technicals

## The IFVG Setup

**Quick Guide Checklist**

- 🕒 **Time:** Trade only between 9:30–11:00am EST (NY Killzone)
    *Why: This window brings the most liquidity and best moves.*
- 📏 **Correct Range:** Ensure price is on the right side of the premium/discount range
    *Premium (for shorts), Discount (for longs). Avoid mid-range trades—align with smart money direction.*
- 💧 **Major Liquidity Sweep:** Wait for a sweep of a major level
    *Examples: London/Asia session high or low, or current day's high/low (LOD/HOD). This signals the manipulation phase.*
- ⚡ **Instant Reaction:** Look for an immediate and strong reaction from the liquidity sweep
    *You want a sharp reversal within 1–2 candles. No stalling—if it lingers, skip the trade.*
- 💥 **Momentum Close:** See a strong momentum candle closing above/below the IFVG within 1–2 candles
    *This confirms a real orderflow shift. Look for large, decisive candles (engulfing, strong body).*
- 🎯 **Clear Targets:** Define your target area before entering
    *Examples: Prior lows/highs, equal highs/lows, or higher timeframe swing points. Predetermined exits = consistency.*

**Self-Assessment Checklist**

- ✅ Did I wait for the killzone window?
- ✅ Was my entry on the correct side of premium/discount?
- ✅ Did a major liquidity sweep happen first?
- ✅ Was there an instant, strong reaction (no stalling)?
- ✅ Did momentum confirm the move within 1–2 candles?
- ✅ Was my target clearly planned before entry?

## When to Go Break Even

- Go break even only after first internal swing high/low is at least 10–20 points in profit.
- Never go break even at tiny 4–5 point swings.
- If price is truly in a buy (or sell) model, it should *not* return to entry after making that first significant swing.
- If price returns to entry after a real 10–20 point move, it's a bad sign.
- Don't choke your A+ setups with tight break evens—let them breathe.

Swing point = highest/lowest candle in a sequence of 3.

---

## How to Find Good Stop Losses on IFVGs

- **Soft stop:** Full candle body close above/below your entry gap = stop out.
- **Hard stop (emergency):** Place below recent swing low/high *only if* price is running hard against you and you need to limit damage before candle close.
- 90% of the time: wait for the candle to close.
- Don't let wicks shake you out.

Why candle close? Prevents getting manipulated out on wicks—price must invalidate your entry with commitment, not just a probe.

---

## How to Trim and Take Profit

- **TP1:** Trim 30–50% at first major swing (20–30 points).
    - If an FVG is just past your TP, take more off (expect rejection).
    - If open road to clear target (e.g., equal highs/lows), trim less, hold more.
- **TP2:** Trim another 20–30% at next swing/clear level.
- **Final TP:** Leave 1–3 micros for main liquidity objective (high/low of day, session high/low, major drawn liquidity).

---

## How to Find the Best IFVG Setups

- Only consider 30s, 1m, 2m, 3m IFVGs for entries.
- Cycle through timeframes *as the sweep is happening* to spot the most attractive gap.
- Consider: gap size, stop distance, break even/trimming areas.
- Do not use 5m+ for entries (too much risk, late confirmation).
- IFVG must be:
    - Big enough for conviction (8–15 points ideal)
    - Has a logical spot to go break even/trim, not just random gaps

---

## How to Find Daily Bias

- Only care about:
    - Overnight highs/lows (London/Asia)
    - Daily range (premium/discount, i.e., 50% line)
- **In premium:** look for shorts to 50% or lower.
- **In discount:** look for longs to 50% or higher.
- If all liquidity swept pre-market and price is at equilibrium: stay cautious—expect chop.
- For PM session, use NY range instead of full daily range.

---

## How Long After a Liquidity Sweep Is the Play Valid?

- Highest win rate: **immediate reactions** after sweep (within a few candles).
- Setups can still be valid 30–40 min later **IF** price doesn't nuke through the sweep level.
- If price blows through sweep with momentum, the sweep is no longer valid—look for the next major level.

---

## Main Reasons IFVGs Fail

- **Bad timing:** Most failures occur outside New York kill zone (9:30–11am ET).
- **Liquidity not swept:** If a major liquidity pool (Asia/London) is still nearby, price may go for it, not your reversal.
- **Gap size:** Too small (under 8 points), setup lacks conviction and structure—ideally 8–15 pts.

---

## How to Trade the 50% Level

- Draw daily/session range from low to high; 50% is your equilibrium.
- In premium, look for shorting opportunities back to 50%.
- In discount, look for longs to 50%.
- 50% is a major TP/trimming area—take heavy partials there.
- After 50% is hit, expect a bounce or reversal—if trend is strong, may continue, but never assume.

---

## How and When to "Chase" an IFVG Entry

- **Standard entry:** Wait for candle close above/below gap for confirmation.
- **Chase Entry:** Enter before candle close when all confluences aligned AND waiting would ruin R:R / move is explosive.
    - Full-size if only a few seconds remain.
    - Half-size if more time remains; add second half after close/retest.
    - Exit quickly if confirmation fails.

## Trading Data Highs & Lows (A+ Setup)

- Red folder news creates a 1-min "data wick" (8:30 or 10am).
- Wait for price to sweep one side of that wick.
- After sweep, take IFVG setup to target the other side.
- Extremely high win rate.

---

## How to Identify Chop and Bad Trading Conditions

- **NQ Range filter:** If AM range < 100 points by 10:30am EST, likely a chop day.
- **15-min Opening Range filter:** If price is still within opening 15-min candle after 2–3 more 15-min candles (by 10–10:30am), likely choppy.
- Look for horizontal, not vertical, price action—if ping-ponging, stand aside.
- Never force A+ setups in bad conditions—edge is gone.

## Liquidity Tier List Ranking

1. Data highs/lows (red folder news wicks)
2. Overnight (London/Asia) highs/lows
3. Previous day high/low
4. 4hr highs/lows
5. 1hr highs/lows
6. New York AM high/low (esp. for PM reversals)

Higher timeframe = more valid sweep.

---

## What is a "Tempo Sweep"?

- A "tempo sweep" (or "fake liquidity sweep") is when price almost hits a major high/low (1–2 points/ticks away) and still reverses hard, as if it swept.
- Optional trade: lower probability than a true sweep.

---

## How to Approach Order Blocks & Find Good Re-Entries

If stopped out at BE but still believe in direction:
1. **Lower TF IFVG re-entry:** drop to 30s/1m, look for a new gap.
2. **FBG Tap:** enter on the 50% of a respected gap (if price respects the level, shows wicks, not closes).
3. **Order Block Entry:** after down candle, bullish engulf; enter on top of body. Acts as cushion/support.

Re-entries are always lower conviction than first A+ setup.

## How to Trade London Session with IFVGs

- Mark Asia session high/low at 2am EST (London open).
- Wait for one side (Asia high/low) to sweep, find IFVG entry, target other side.
- Fallback if Asia messy: previous day H/L or hourly/4hr H/L.

London session 2am–5am EST.

---

## How to Trade the NW PM Session

- Trade PM only if no AM setup found, or lightly red.
- If green, don't touch PM.
- At 11am EST, mark hourly H/L and NY AM H/L (9:30–11:00).
- Wait for sweep, find IFVG, target opposite side.

PM setups have lower winrate than AM.
